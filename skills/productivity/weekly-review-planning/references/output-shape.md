# Weekly Review and Planning — Output Shape

**Goal:** the review produces a structured report in seven sections.

## Required sections

1. Wins and completed commitments
2. Overdue or at risk
3. Waiting/follow-ups
4. Stalled or ambiguous projects
5. Next week's outcomes and calendar constraints
6. Proposed updates awaiting approval
7. Coverage gaps

## Rules

- Sections 1–5 are factual; section 6 is the approval gate; section 7 states what was not covered.
- A section with no content still appears with "None."
- The report is the artifact; mutations happen only in step 7 after approval.

## Completion

- All seven sections are present in the report.
- Each stalled/waiting flag traces to a specific record, event, or thread.
